Scam shield

Real contests NEVER ask for payment, card data or a PIN. Check that the page belongs to the Georgia station. The stale star941fm.iheart.com and v103.iheart.com domains redirect to San Diego stations, not Atlanta.

Wrong-station traps: b985radio.com is a New Jersey station and b985.fm is Maine — neither is Atlanta’s B98.5. star941fm.iheart.com is San Diego, not Atlanta’s Star 94.1.

offerscontest.com is NOT official. It is circulating a “WSB-TV Gas/Grocery giveaway” page as a third-party aggregator. Real WSB-TV contests are on wsbtv.com only.

Use only station promotion pages, station apps and official rules linked in this scout.

Radio One never charges for airplay. Scammers clone station or personality pages, steal logos and photos, then DM artists asking for payment through an app. This warning applies to Hot 107.9 and Majic 107.5.

The unofficial “Iheart 1000 Contest” PDF on quantumcourse.iitr.ac.in is still circulating, and a copy now sits at virtual.cteg.edu.py. Do not download or trust either. Aggregator details on freebiemom.com are NOT official — the same goes for contestreminder, sweepsadvantage and sweepstakesfanatics — and never provide card details to enter an iHeart sweepstakes.

Standing caution: a WJIZ-branded redirect through clementine-anma.xyz was reported before Oct 19. Enter only through official iheart.com or station pages.

A sites.google.com page promising a $750–$1,000 Cash App win surfaced in Haunted Cash searches — it is not a station page; do not enter. Standing warning: enter Haunted Cash only on official station domains. A news-usa.today article about an Albany iHeart giveaway is not an official iHeart page.

Augusta trap: hot977.com is a PARKED GoDaddy domain for sale ($7,999) — it is NOT Hot 97.7 Augusta’s site (the real site is hotaugusta.com). Any contest contact from hot977.com is suspect. hot97.com is New York’s HOT 97, not Augusta.

iHeart Q4 $1,000 is OVER: the iHeartRadio 2026 Q4 Cash Sweepstakes ended Fri Nov 13, 2026 at 9:54:59 PM ET. No successor iHeart cash-keyword contest was found Nov 16 — anyone still collecting “hourly keywords” for it is not running a live contest. The fake PDFs on quantumcourse.iitr.ac.in and virtual.cteg.edu.py were not seen circulating this week, but the warning stands. freebiemom.com is a non-official aggregator (its Give Thanks & Win and Q4 Cash pages were updated Nov 15–16) — enter only through official station pages or the iHeartRadio app. offerscontest.com is also a non-official aggregator, not a scam.

Cash App fake — standing warning: the old “Cash App 750” sites.google.com page still exists in the search index (last crawl ~293 days ago) and did NOT re-surface as a new item this week. Real giveaways never ask for payment, passwords, or sign-in codes.

Context, not scams: some CMG and Hot 107.9 contest links mis-route to privacy or agency pages. Haunted Cash is an official SoCast Digital network-wide partner promotion; its entry form is hosted by SoCast, not the station.
